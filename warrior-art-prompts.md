## MASTER PROMPT (identical for all 35)

```
CHARACTER: <paste the character line for the file you are generating>

Full-body fantasy game hero, standing heroic idle pose, weapon in hand,
3/4 view facing slightly to the right, entire body visible from head to feet.

Art style: cohesive mobile-game hero illustration, semi-realistic painterly
with clean cel-shading, bold readable silhouette, crisp rim light, rich
saturated colors, subtle metallic highlights, detailed face and gear.

Composition: SINGLE character, centered, full body, feet at the very bottom
edge, small even margin around the figure, consistent scale, no props floating.

Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (alpha PNG). No scenery, no ground, no shadow.

Lighting: soft key light from top-left, cool rim light on the right edge.

Output: 512x640 portrait, sharp, no blur.

Avoid: background, scenery, ground, text, watermark, signature, borders,
frame, multiple characters, cropped limbs, extra weapons, motion blur, photorealism.
```
