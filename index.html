Battle Codex

⚔️ THE ARENA

Two stone arenas stack vertically. Enemies emerge from the Hell Gate and march along the marked path to the Golden Gate. Warriors on the 4×4 tiles auto-attack enemies in range.

💧 ELIXIR & SUMMONING

  • Elixir regenerates over time (faster in the endless phase) — max 10
  • Summon cost by rarity: Common 2 · Uncommon/Rare 3 · Unique 4 · Epic 5 · Legendary 6
  • Wave cleared: +2💧 · Next Wave button: +2💧 · Boss slain: full refill
  • Drag a warrior to the bottom strip to SELL it for half its cost

✨ ULTIMATES & TARGETING

  • Attacks and kills charge each warrior's ultimate meter
  • Tap a glowing warrior to unleash its ultimate (see its card)
  • Tap a warrior anytime to cycle targeting: Nearest → Strongest → First
  • Bosses are always focused when in range

👹 KNOW YOUR MONSTERS

  • Shieldbearer: takes half damage — unless the hit is a critical
  • Gargoyle: flies past roots, slows and knockback
  • Shaman: heals nearby monsters — hunt it first (try First/Strongest targeting)
  • Slime: splits into two on death
  • A named Champion marches every 5th wave

✨ MERGING

  • Drag a warrior onto one of the same color and stars
  • Merged warrior: +65% atk, faster speed, +16 range
  • Maximum 4 stars — shown above the warrior

🪙 GOLD REWARDS

  • Goblin: +5 · Skeleton: +10 · Orc: +20 · Boss: +500
  • Wave cleared: +25 · Next Wave button: +15

🏆 VICTORY & SURVIVAL RECORDS

Break your rival's base to win — then SURVIVAL MODE begins: the horde keeps coming for you alone, growing fiercer forever. The run ends when your base falls, recording your best wave. Beat it every run! Breach = -1 to -3 HP.

⬆️ WARRIOR LEVELS

  • XP comes ONLY from kills — each warrior earns 5 XP per enemy IT kills in battle
  • Or Enhance: sacrifice spare recruits — XP rises steeply with the sacrifice's level & rarity
  • Growth follows the role: Warriors +14% atk/lvl · Archers +5% speed & +4 range/lvl · Mages +18% atk/lvl · Tanks balanced
  • Abilities grow +6% potency per level, with milestones every 5 levels

🎲 RECRUITS & RARITY

  • Every recruit is a unique copy: rolled rarity plus rolled attack, speed and range
  • Rarity bonus: Common +0% · Uncommon +10% · Rare +25% · Unique +45% · Epic +70% · Legendary +110%
  • Legendary odds: 1.5% — a Legendary is mighty from Level 1
  • Different-hero duplicates are fuel: feed them for XP via Enhance

🏰 CASTLE UPGRADES

  • Spend gold on permanent base upgrades (Castle Upgrades in the main menu)
  • Golden Gate: +2 base HP per level — and your fortress visibly grows on the battlefield
  • Elixir Well: faster elixir regen per level, +1 starting elixir every 3 levels

⭐ ASCENSION & TALENTS

  • Fuse a duplicate of the SAME warrior to Ascend: +15% stats and +10% ability power per star, up to 5★ — permanent
  • At Lv5 choose one of two Talents per warrior; every later milestone (Lv10, Lv15…) deepens your chosen talent automatically

✦ UNIQUE ABILITIES

  • Every warrior has a signature ability, shown on their card
  • Auras (Leonidas, Joan, Sun Tzu) buff only the allies on the tiles directly touching them — above, below and to the sides. Place them centrally!
  • On-hit effects: splash, chain lightning, poison, burns, curses, roots, knockback, crits
  • Auras from the same warrior don't stack, but different auras combine

⚡ STATUS REACTIONS

Two elements on one enemy combine into a bonus payoff — pair the right warriors (or Frost Nova) to trigger them:

  • 🧊 Shatter — hit a Frozen foe (Frost Nova): huge bonus damage + armor stripped
  • 💥 Corrode — Burn (Khan/Ra) + Acid (Alchemist): armor-melting blast
  • ⚡ Conduct — Chain Lightning (Merlin) hits harder & farther on Rooted/Frozen foes
  • 🌿 Overgrowth — Rooted/Frozen foes take +50% from splash & cleave
  • ☠️ Toxic Bloom — a Cursed (Morgana) + Poisoned (Cleopatra) foe erupts poison on death
